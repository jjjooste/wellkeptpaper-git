Privacy

Last updated: 16 October 2026. This page explains what we collect on this site and why. We follow the New Zealand Privacy Act 2020.

What we collect

When you join the early-bird list, we collect your email address, your first name if you give it, your consent tick, whether you ticked the optional pre-order interest box, and a campaign tag showing which page version you signed up on.

Why

We use it only to send you updates about the Well Kept Paper planner launch and the early-bird offer. We don't sell your details or use them for anything else. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we remove you promptly (Unsolicited Electronic Messages Act 2007).

Where it's stored

This site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. When you sign up, your details are sent by our form service, FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), to our email inbox. Both may process data outside New Zealand, including in the United States. If we later move the list to an email service, we'll update this page.

Advertising pixel (optional)

We may use the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) pixel to measure whether our ads lead to signups. If it's enabled, it records page visits and a "Lead" event when you sign up, and Meta may set cookies. We don't send your email address to Meta. You can manage ad preferences in your Meta account settings or block third-party cookies in your browser. Pixel status: not enabled.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your details at any time by replying to any email we send you. If you're not happy with our response, you can contact the Office of the Privacy Commissioner (privacy.org.nz).

How long we keep it

Until you unsubscribe or ask us to delete it, or until the planner launch campaign ends, whichever comes first.
