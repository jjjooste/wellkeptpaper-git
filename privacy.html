What we collect

When you join the early-bird list, we collect your email address, your first name if you give it, your consent tick, whether you ticked the optional pre-order interest box, and a campaign tag showing which page version you signed up on.

Why

We use it only to send you updates about the Well Kept Paper planner launch and the early-bird offer. We don't sell your details or use them for anything else. Every email has an unsubscribe link, and we remove you promptly.

Where it's stored

This site is hosted by Cloudflare, Inc. When you sign up, your details are sent by our form service, FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), to our email inbox. Both may process data in other countries, including the United States. If we later move the list to an email service, we'll update this page.

Where your details are transferred outside the UK, we rely on our providers' standard safeguards for international transfers.

Where your details are transferred outside the EEA, we rely on our providers' safeguards for international transfers, such as Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework.

Advertising pixel (optional)

We may use the Meta (Facebook/Instagram) pixel to measure whether our ads lead to signups. If it's enabled, it records page visits and a "Lead" event when you sign up, and Meta may set cookies. We don't send your email address to Meta. You can manage ad preferences in your Meta account settings or block third-party cookies in your browser. Pixel status: not enabled.
