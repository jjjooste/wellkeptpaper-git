Printing

  • Print on A4, portrait, at "actual size" or "fit to page".
  • Print only the pages you need, and reprint pages as often as you like for your household.
  • Hole-punch the pages and keep them in a binder or on a clipboard.

Using it digitally

  • Open the PDF in a note-taking app such as GoodNotes, Notability or Xodo and write on the pages with a stylus.
  • Keep the original file untouched and work on a copy, so you always have clean pages.

Getting started

Fill in Year at a Glance and Bills & Due Dates first, then set aside ten minutes a week to keep everything up to date.
